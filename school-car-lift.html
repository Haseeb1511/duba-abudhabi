School & Campus FAQs

Do you pick up directly from our home?

Yes. We offer door-to-door pickups across major Dubai residential hubs (Dubai Marina, JLT, Al Barsha, Downtown, Business Bay, Silicon Oasis, JVC, and more) and drop off right at the school or university gate in Abu Dhabi (ADGM, Corniche, Khalifa City, Al Reem Island, Zayed City).

Can teachers and university faculty join?

Absolutely. Many international school teachers and university professors living in Dubai and teaching in Abu Dhabi rely on our daily morning (7:20 AM) and evening return (3:30 PM & 4:40 PM) shifts to commute without the stress of daily highway driving.

What happens during school holidays and term breaks?

Our monthly packages can be adjusted or paused with advance notice for scheduled winter, spring, and summer academic term breaks.
