Private Car Lift FAQs

How many passengers and bags can fit in the private vehicle?

Our fleet features spacious 7-seater MPVs (Kia Carnival, Honda Odyssey, Toyota Previa) and full-size SUVs (Toyota Grand Highlander). They comfortably accommodate 1 to 6 passengers with multiple large suitcases and work bags.

Are Salik tolls and fuel included in the quoted fare?

Yes. All private quotes are agreed upfront with tolls, fuel, and door-to-door highway travel included so there are zero surprise meter fees.

Can I request multiple stops along the way?

Yes. Because the entire vehicle is booked exclusively for you, you can request additional stops (such as dropping off a colleague at ADGM before proceeding to the Corniche) for a small pre-agreed supplement.
