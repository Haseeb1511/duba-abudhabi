Driver Standards FAQs

Are all drivers commercially licensed in the UAE?

Yes. Every chauffeur in our team holds valid UAE commercial passenger driver permits with verified credentials and clean highway safety records.

How do drivers assist with luggage and passenger comfort?

Chauffeurs handle all loading and unloading of luggage, pre-cool the vehicle interior before arrival, and adapt to passenger climate and privacy preferences.

Do you assign the same driver for daily and monthly commute plans?

Yes. For our scheduled school, corporate, and ladies car lift packages, you are assigned permanent dedicated drivers to ensure consistent timing and peace of mind.
