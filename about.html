Why We Focus Exclusively on Dubai ⇄ Abu Dhabi

Commuting between Dubai and Abu Dhabi every day is a serious drive—around 140 to 150 km each way along Sheikh Zayed Road (E11). Many transport services try to cover every city across the UAE all at once, which often leads to delayed pickups and stressed drivers. Instead, we focus on doing one thing well: providing reliable, on-time daily rides strictly between Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

By keeping our focus on this single route, our drivers know the traffic patterns, the morning bottlenecks, and the quickest ways into key business and residential hubs like Downtown Dubai, Marina, DIFC, ADGM, and central Abu Dhabi.

5+ Years on the Road

Over the past 5 years, we have carried more than 5,000 riders safely between Dubai and Abu Dhabi. From consultants and lawyers commuting to Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) to university students and corporate staff, our riders depend on us to get them to their destinations on time.

Because our drivers make this trip twice a day, every day, they know exactly how traffic moves during peak hours, how to handle tolls (Salik and Darb) smoothly, and how to keep your ride comfortable from door to door.

Door-to-Door Coverage in Dubai & Abu Dhabi

Our service provides convenient door-to-door pick and drop without the inconvenience of navigating busy bus stations or multiple transit transfers:

  • Dubai Pickups: Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, DIFC, Dubai Marina, JLT, Al Barsha, Deira, Bur Dubai, Silicon Oasis, and Dubai Investment Park (DIP).
  • Abu Dhabi Drop-Offs: Abu Dhabi Corniche, Downtown, Al Reem Island, Al Maryah Island (ADGM), Khalifa City, MBZ City, Musaffah, and ADNEC.
  • Custom pickup points and specific drop zones can be arranged when booking.

Our Commute Solutions

We provide two distinct travel modes tailored to your timetable and budget:

  1. Shared Carpooling (One-Way & Round-Trip): Budget-conscious daily commuters share a modern, air-conditioned vehicle with fellow professionals. Single one-way seats begin at AED 150, and same-day Round Trip seats start from AED 220.
  2. Dedicated Private & Solo Car Lift: Private door-to-door transport exclusively for you or your group, offering flexible departure times, non-stop direct travel, and dedicated comfort. One-way private rides start from AED 250, and Private Round Trips with waiting time start from AED 450.
  3. Commuter Packages: Flexible Weekly Passes (Mon–Fri, from AED 550) and Monthly Office Commuter Subscriptions (Mon–Fri, from AED 2,500) designed for predictable daily office work travel with substantial cost savings.

Safety & Fleet Standards

All our vehicles are clean, spacious, and air-conditioned—mostly family MPVs and SUVs like the Kia Carnival and Toyota Grand Highlander. Every car is regularly inspected, serviced, and kept fresh inside. Our drivers hold valid UAE commercial licenses, have clean safety records, and treat passengers with courtesy and respect.
