Quote requests and bookings

A message or quote request sent through this website is an inquiry and does not, by itself, reserve a ride. A booking is confirmed only after you and the service agree on the trip details.

Confirm the trip details

Before booking, confirm the pickup and drop-off locations, travel date and time, ride type, vehicle, final fare, fare inclusions, and any other charges or conditions that apply to your trip.

Starting fares

Any fares displayed on the website are starting figures. The service should give you the full fare for your specific trip and confirm it before you book.

Vehicle photos

Vehicle photos on this website are representative examples from Wikimedia Commons. They may differ from the actual vehicle provided. Ask which vehicle will be used for your ride.

Changes and cancellations

Ask the service about cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms before confirming a booking. Any agreed conditions should be clearly stated in the booking confirmation.

Contact

For questions about a quote or these terms, message the service on WhatsApp. Email is available as a reference contact at dubaitoabudhabilift@gmail.com.
