Terms of Service & Commuter Policies

Clear, transparent rules for all daily, weekly, and monthly passengers traveling between Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

1. Terms of Service & Booking

All bookings with Dubai to Abu Dhabi Lift are coordinated directly via official WhatsApp and phone channels. By booking a ride, passengers agree to the following terms:

  • All-Inclusive Pricing: Every quote provided covers fuel, Salik toll charges, and vehicle driver fees. No hidden surge charges are applied once a booking is confirmed.
  • Punctuality: Commuters are requested to be ready at their designated building entrance or pickup area 5 minutes prior to the scheduled departure time to maintain route schedules for all passengers.
  • Driver & Vehicle Information: Driver details, vehicle model, and registration plate numbers are communicated via WhatsApp prior to trip commencement.
  • Luggage Allowance: Standard sedans accommodate up to 2 medium-to-large suitcases. Passengers traveling with oversized baggage must notify dispatch when booking to assign an SUV or minivan.

2. Cancellation, Pauses & Refunds

We understand that work schedules change. Our cancellation rules are designed to be commuter-friendly:

  • Daily Rides: Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to scheduled departure time receive a 100% full refund or free rescheduling to another date.
  • Same-Day Cancellations: Notice given less than 4 hours before pickup may incur a 50% cancellation fee to compensate the driver assigned to that route.
  • Monthly Subscription Pauses: Monthly pass holders can freeze or pause their subscription (e.g., for annual leave or official business trips) by providing 3 days advance notice. Days paused will be credited forward to the subsequent billing month.

3. Accepted Payment Methods

Flexible payment methods are supported for both individual commuters and corporate accounts:

  • Cash: Payable directly to your verified driver at trip pickup or drop-off.
  • Bank Transfer: UAE local bank transfer (instant transfer via IBAN/App).
  • Corporate Invoicing: Official monthly tax invoices provided for registered corporate and staff transport accounts.

4. Safety & Passenger Standards

All vehicles in our network are non-smoking, clean, fully insured, and climate-controlled. Seatbelts must be worn at all times in compliance with UAE traffic regulations. We also offer dedicated women-only carpool vehicles upon request for female commuters seeking added privacy and comfort.
