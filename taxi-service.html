Taxi Service FAQs

How does this taxi service compare to city metered taxis?

Unlike standard metered city taxis where intercity fares can fluctuate drastically due to traffic congestion, route detours, or crossing fees, we give you an upfront, fixed all-inclusive price. You also get door-to-door reservation in advance so there is no waiting in taxi stands.

Are tolls (Salik in Dubai and Darb in Abu Dhabi) included in the price?

Yes. All stated taxi fares include all mandatory road tolls along the E11 Sheikh Zayed Road and E311 corridors, including Dubai Salik gates and Abu Dhabi Darb tolls. There are no additional surprises.

Can I book a taxi in the middle of the night or early morning?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For late-night or early dawn departures (e.g. 3:00 AM to 6:00 AM), we recommend booking 2 to 4 hours in advance so our dispatch assigns your chauffeur without delay.

What vehicles are used for the intercity taxi service?

We maintain high-standard comfort vehicles including clean sedans (such as Toyota Camry, Nissan Altima) and spacious MPVs and SUVs (such as Kia Carnival, Toyota Previa, Honda Odyssey) for passengers traveling with extensive luggage or family members.
