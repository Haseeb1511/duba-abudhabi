Privacy notice

This notice describes what the website currently does when you browse or prepare a ride inquiry.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Ride inquiry form

The form opens WhatsApp with your name, phone number, and trip details in a draft message. The form details are not submitted to this website or stored by it. The message is sent only after you review it and choose Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp and the service handle the message under their own privacy practices.

When the business WhatsApp number is configured, the message opens addressed to the service. Until then, WhatsApp opens the message so you can choose a chat. Review WhatsApp’s privacy information before using it.

Website storage and tracking

This site currently does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or cookie-based tracking. Your browser makes a normal connection to the website host when you view a page; the host may process standard technical request logs under its own terms.

Information in your inquiry

The form requires a name, phone number, pickup area, and destination so the service can follow up about your request. Email is available as a reference contact at dubaitoabudhabilift@gmail.com.

Changes to this notice

This page should be updated if the website later adds a booking system, analytics, advertising, or other tools that collect information.
