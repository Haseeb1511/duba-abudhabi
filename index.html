Frequently Asked Questions About Car Lift Dubai to Abu Dhabi

Are fuel and Salik toll charges included in the car lift fare?

Yes, 100% of fuel and all Salik toll charges along the E11 Sheikh Zayed Road are included in every quote. The price confirmed before your trip is the exact amount you pay with zero hidden fees or surge pricing.

How much does a monthly car lift from Dubai to Abu Dhabi cost?

Monthly shared car lift packages start from AED 2,500 covering Monday through Friday (20 to 22 working days) with morning pickup and evening return. Private dedicated monthly car lift packages start from AED 6,000.

How long is the travel time between Dubai and Abu Dhabi?

The door-to-door drive typically takes between 75 to 90 minutes along the E11 Sheikh Zayed Road corridor. During peak weekday commute hours, please allow an extra 10 to 15 minutes.

What is the difference between shared car lift and private car lift?

Shared car lift matches you with up to 3 co-passengers traveling on the same time window and area, offering maximum cost savings. Private car lift gives you exclusive use of the vehicle with departure at your exact chosen time.

Can I book a return ride from Abu Dhabi to Dubai?

Yes. We operate scheduled evening return departures from Abu Dhabi to Dubai between 3:30 PM and 6:00 PM daily. Round-trip bookings receive discounted rates compared to two individual one-way rides.

Do you offer airport transfers between Dubai (DXB/DWC) and Abu Dhabi (AUH)?

Yes. We provide scheduled and private intercity airport transfers with real-time flight tracking and ample luggage capacity in sedans, SUVs, and vans.

Is there a ladies-only car lift option available?

Yes. Female passengers can request a ladies-only shared ride when booking via WhatsApp, ensuring all co-passengers in the vehicle are women.

Which areas in Dubai and Abu Dhabi are covered for door-to-door pickup?

In Dubai, we cover Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay, Downtown, Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Nahda, Al Barsha, JVC, Silicon Oasis, and Dubai Airport. In Abu Dhabi, drop-offs include Abu Dhabi City Centre, Khalifa City, Mussafah, Yas Island, Reem Island, Maryah Island, Corniche, and Abu Dhabi Airport.

What happens if I need to cancel or change my booking?

Cancellations or schedule adjustments made at least 24 hours prior to pickup are 100% free of charge. Monthly commuters can also pause their subscription for annual leave by notifying us 3 days in advance.

How do I book a ride and make payment?

Booking requires no complicated app downloads—simply send a message on WhatsApp with your pickup location, desired time, and shared or private preference. Payment is accepted via cash to the driver or instant UAE bank transfer.
