Good to know

Questions before you book

Check trip details directly before confirming.

What are the scheduled morning and evening office shift timings?

We run structured daily departures (Monday to Friday) matching office hours:
• Morning Departures (Dubai to Abu Dhabi): 7:30 AM, 8:00 AM, 8:30 AM, and 9:00 AM
• Evening Return Shifts (Abu Dhabi to Dubai): 3:30 PM, 4:00 PM, 4:30 PM, 5:00 PM, and 6:00 PM
Custom departure times can also be tailored for private single, weekly, and monthly bookings.

Which vehicles are listed as available?

Kia Carnival, Kia Sedona, Honda Odyssey, Toyota Grand Highlander, Toyota Previa, Toyota Sienna, Nissan Altima, and Nissan Quest. Ask which model will be assigned to your ride.

Are the listed fares final?

No. The amounts on this page are starting fares. Ask for the full fare for your pickup, destination, date, ride type, and any return trip, and confirm it before booking.

Are tolls and fuel included?

Yes. Salik and Darb tolls and highway fuel are included in our fares, and your confirmed quote will state this clearly before you book.

How long will the journey take?

Usually 75–90 minutes door-to-door along the E11. The exact time depends on your pickup and drop-off areas and traffic on the day.

How do I request a ride?

Fill in the trip details below and tap Book on WhatsApp. Review the message and press Send in WhatsApp. A booking is confirmed only after the service agrees the details with you.

What is the cancellation or change policy?

Ask for the cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms before confirming your booking.
